# Two-lane SE engagement pilot (30 days)

**Goal:** spend SE time where it changes outcomes.

- **Lane 1, exploratory.** A short qualification step, then a reusable, on-demand product
  experience. No SE time until the qualification gaps close.
- **Lane 2, qualified and complex.** A named SE owner, tailored discovery and agreed
  technical success criteria.

**Measure, per lane:**
1. SE hours per qualified opportunity
2. Demo-to-next-stage conversion
3. Time to technical decision (median days)

**Day 30:** compare the lanes, then adjust coverage. Raise or lower the lane-2 threshold, and
change what the lane-1 experience covers based on where deals stall.

**Data:** log each deal in `pilot.csv` at entry and update the dates as stages move.
