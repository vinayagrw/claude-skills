# 7-slide pitch outline

1. **Title.** "{Our company} × {Company}: {one-line value}". Add a footer with the person, their role and the date.
2. **What we heard.** Three cards, one per stated priority, each with the fact that backs it.
3. **The market is moving.** Three big numbers or facts: their headroom, plus two competitor moves.
4. **How we help.** Four offer cards in a 2×2 grid. Use a placeholder for each case study.
5. **AI opportunities and roadmap.** Today vs target, the top 3 AI opportunities and a 0 to 6, 6 to 12 and 12 to 24 month roadmap.
6. **What a pilot looks like.** Assess (2 weeks) → pilot (≤90 days) → success measures.
7. **Next step.** The proposed assessment, plus 3 scoping questions, on an accent background.

Every slide gets speaker notes. Body text is at least 24px. On delivery, list the number and title of every slide.
