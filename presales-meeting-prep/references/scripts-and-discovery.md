# Discovery and scripts

## Discovery by lane
- **Lane 1 (exploratory):** 5 questions, 15 minutes. Is there a real pain, who owns it, is
  there a date, is there budget, who else decides. Then point to the on-demand experience.
- **Lane 2 (qualified):** full discovery. Current state, target state, metrics, decision
  criteria and process, integrations, security and data constraints, then agree the
  technical success criteria in writing.

Each question gets a "why we ask" line and what a good and a weak answer sound like.

## Script rules
- Open with their world, not our product: one fact from the research and one pain.
- One ask per message. Short lines. Match the persona's words from the persona card.
- Email 1 first touch (under 90 words). Email 2 follow-up with a proof point (under 70).
  Email 3 after the demo, recap with agreed next step and date (under 120).
- Voicemail under 20 seconds. Call opener under 30 seconds, then a question.
- No em-dashes or semicolons. No invented customer names or numbers.
