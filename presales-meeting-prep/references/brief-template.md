# {Company}: meeting brief for {Person}
{Role} · prepared {date} · sources are in `research/`

## 1. Snapshot
- **Person:** career, likely reporting line, public voice. Say so if their public posting is thin.
- **Company:** 4 to 5 settled facts from 00-facts.md, strategy, recent wins or deals relevant to their priorities.
- **Their area:** the product or platform they own and its state, including what has slipped.

## 2. Live signals
Active hiring (count and date checked) and leader posts from the last 30 days, with what each signals.

## 3. What they are likely dealing with
3 to 4 pain hypotheses, each combining evidence from at least two research files.

## 4. Today vs target
One paragraph and a simple flow for each: how it works today, and the target state.

## 5. Top 5 challenges
A table per product or area: challenge today, challenge tomorrow, how to do it differently.

## 6. AI opportunities and roadmap
Top 5 AI opportunities, then the 0 to 6, 6 to 12 and 12 to 24 month roadmap.

## 7. Competitive landscape
Table of 3 direct, 3 industry leaders and 3 startups: what they do, latest AI move, threat to {Company}. Note alternates.

## 8. Industry news mapped
Top 5 items from the weekly reports and live news, each as risk, opportunity or talk track. State report dates.

## 9. Our offers, mapped to their priorities
Table: their priority, what we propose. Include one hidden pain and one next-horizon item.

## 10. Discovery questions
10 to 12 open questions, each testing one hypothesis.

## 11. Talking points
Lead with domain depth. Tie points to their background. No invented case studies.

## 12. Likely objections
Table: what they might say, our answer.

## 13. Proposed next step
A small fixed-scope assessment or pilot, with success measures.

## 14. Facts and conflicts
Facts settled during verification and anything still unresolved.

## 15. Avoid
- third-party claims stated as fact
- implying we have read their posts when we haven't
- topics outside their remit
- timing traps such as an earnings date or a reorg
