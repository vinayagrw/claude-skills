# Proposal outline

1. Bid or no-bid box: coverage, red count, deadline risk, fit score
2. Executive summary: the buyer's problem in their words, our answer, 3 win themes
3. Understanding of requirements: mapped to their numbered requirements
4. Solution: architecture diagram (style picker), how it meets each requirement
5. Why us vs the alternatives: from the battle card, no named attacks on competitors
6. Delivery plan and technical success criteria (lane 2 deals)
7. Security and compliance: links to the answered questionnaire
8. Commercials: placeholders only, filled by the account owner
9. Assumptions and open questions
